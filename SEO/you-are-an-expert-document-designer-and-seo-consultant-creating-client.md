3. What is SEO?
SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is improving your website to rank higher in Google search results for relevant searches. It includes:
- On-Page SEO: Keywords in titles/headings/content, quality writing, images with alt text.
- Off-Page SEO: Earning backlinks from trusted sites.
- Technical SEO: Fast loading, mobile-friendly, secure (HTTPS), crawlable structure.
Results take 3–6+ months but last long-term.

7. Why Your Business Needs a Google Business Profile (and What It Does)
Google Business Profile (GBP, formerly Google My Business) is a **free** tool that controls how your business appears on Google Search and Maps.

What it does:
- Shows key info: name, address, phone (NAP), hours, website, photos, services/products, menu (if applicable)
- Collects & displays customer reviews/ratings, Q&A
- Lets you post updates, offers, events
- Provides insights: how people find you, calls/directions/website clicks
- Supports messaging, booking links

Why every local business needs it in 2026:
- Powers local SEO ("near me" searches, Google Map Pack)
- Acts as your 24/7 digital storefront – often first thing seen
- Builds trust with reviews/photos/accurate info
- Drives actions: 45% more directions with photos, 31% more website clicks
- Free high-ROI – optimized profiles rank higher
- Critical in AI search (Google uses GBP data in answers/overviews)
- Without it, competitors steal your local traffic
